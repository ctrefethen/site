My Story

Who I am

I am a third year Mechanical Engineering Student at the University of Colorado Boulder. I have a particular interest in learning the manufacturing side of engineering, bringing complex designs into the real world. I enjoy taking leadership roles in various projects here at CU, and I use my technical know-how to coordinate the process of design to fabrication.

In 2016, I saw my first 3D printer, and was hooked. I saved up enough so later that year, I bought my very own. From there, I learned how to 3D model basic components to make myself, introducing me into the world of mechanical engineering.

Ultimately, I would like to work in mechatronics, designing systems that integrate both mechanical and electronic hardware. Having my own startup developing products is my end-goal. For now, I would like to build as many interesting parts at the machine shop, to build my knowledge of manufacturing capabilities.
