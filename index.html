Research Focus

What is iCLIP?

Injection Continuous Liquid Interface Production — iCLIP — is a breakthrough 3D printing process that eliminates the layer-by-layer limitations of traditional additive manufacturing. By combining continuous photopolymerization with injected resin delivery, iCLIP grows objects from liquid in one uninterrupted motion, achieving speeds and surface qualities far beyond conventional methods.

This site presents original research exploring how iCLIP works, what the literature says about its capabilities, and why it may represent one of the most significant leaps forward in manufacturing since the invention of 3D printing itself. You will find a full academic synthesis and a popular science article written for a general audience — both designed to make this research accessible to anyone curious about the future of making things.

01

Continuous Growth

Unlike FDM or SLA, iCLIP never stops to cure a layer. Parts are pulled upward from a resin pool continuously, producing smooth geometry and far faster build times.

02

Speed & Resolution

Studies show iCLIP can produce parts orders of magnitude faster than standard SLA while maintaining fine geometric detail and strong mechanical properties.

03

Real-World Impact

From biomedical implants to aerospace hardware, iCLIP's precision and throughput are opening doors that layer-based printing simply cannot reach.
